The lowest headline fare isn't always the best trip. Use this checklist to compare flight options, avoid unpleasant connection surprises, and arrive ready to explore.

1. Start with a flexible search

Search Toronto to Bangkok for your intended dates. Compare nearby dates if you can, then note the complete itinerary—not just the lowest number. Check which Toronto and Bangkok airports are shown and whether each result is one-way or round trip.

If you aren't ready to buy, Google Flights lets you track prices for selected dates or, where available, any dates. See Google's tracking instructions.

2. Compare the trip, not just the price

For each option, write down the following in the same currency:

  • Total fare: check the final price, taxes and any payment charges before paying.
  • Baggage: compare the actual carry-on and checked-bag rules for your fare and each flight. Fees can change the ranking.
  • Stops and total travel time: read the full itinerary, including long overnight layovers and airport changes.
  • Connection protection: check whether flights are on one ticket or separate tickets. Separate tickets can mean more responsibility if a delay causes a missed connection.
  • Changes and support: compare cancellation and change rules, and identify whether the airline or an agency will service the booking.
Tip: Build a shortlist of two or three itineraries before choosing. The cheapest option may have a longer connection, a different baggage allowance or more restrictive terms.

The Canadian Transportation Agency explains baggage rules for certain connecting itineraries. Always check the rules shown for your own booking.

3. Plan the first day

  • Check which airport your flight reaches and how you'll get to your accommodation at your actual arrival time.
  • Confirm your accommodation's check-in time and keep its address accessible offline.
  • Compare your mobile carrier's roaming terms with a local SIM or a compatible travel eSIM. Confirm that your phone is unlocked and supports the option you choose.
  • Review the Government of Canada's current Thailand travel advice and entry requirements before booking and again before departure.

4. Before you pay

  1. Check that your name, travel dates and airports are correct.
  2. Review the baggage allowance, connection details and fare conditions.
  3. Confirm the final amount and currency, and save your booking confirmation.
